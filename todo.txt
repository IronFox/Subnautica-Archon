energy health concept:

health:
+ regular sub self repair requires module
	- consumes moderate amount of energy
+ emergency self repair (with or without module) sets in at < 1% health
	- consumes high amount of energy
	- repairs to 5% health
	- sub invulnerable while <1% health (will require testing)
	- remain mobile if sufficient energy remains
	- may kill batteries

energy:
+ cricial power when batteries dead or removed
	- critical power:
		lighting dim pulsing red, light strips pulse red
		external lights offline
		oxygen production offline
		non-emergency self repair suspended (emergency repair _will_ work)
		mobility suspended (can still emergency teleport and auto-level)
		
light strip:
	+ high power: white
	+ <50%, >10%: yellowish
	+ <10%: orange-red
charging: slow pulse
